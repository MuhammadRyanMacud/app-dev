
# My Favorite Movie: Transformers

## About the Movie
My favorite movie is **Transformers**. It is an action and science fiction movie about robots that can transform into vehicles.

## My Favorite Characters
- **Optimus Prime** - The leader of the Autobots.
- *Bumblebee* - A brave and loyal Autobot.
- **Megatron** - The leader of the Decepticons.

## My Favorite Tagline
> "Autobots, let's roll out!"

## Why I Like This Movie
I like Transformers because it has exciting action scenes, amazing robots, and awesome battles. I also like Optimus Prime because he is brave and protects humanity.
